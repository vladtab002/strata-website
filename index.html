About STRATA

Great brands aren’t made in one go. They’re built up, layer on layer, until they hold.

STRATA started with a simple question: why does so much marketing in Romania look the same? We felt the industry here had been standing still, and the work of creatives abroad gave us the push to do something about it, with branding that feels fresh, experimental and, above all, layered.

We’re a small team, and we work the same way with every client. Big or small, arriving with a detailed plan or just an idea to explore, the starting point never changes: a first conversation where we set the direction, the goals and the resources. That’s the first layer, the foundation, and we believe it’s the part that matters most.

From there we build, keeping you updated at a steady pace. You stay in control of every decision. We bring the vision.
