6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access);
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete (rectification);
  • delete your data when we no longer need it or have no legal basis to keep it (erasure);
  • limit how we use it while a question is being resolved (restriction);
  • send it to you or another company in a common format (portability);
  • stop using it for purposes based on our legitimate interest, including follow-up messages (objection).

Write to hello@stratastudio.ro to use any of these rights. We reply within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you think we have handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP (Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal), at dataprotection.ro. We would appreciate the chance to fix it with you first.
